Vendored copy of Matt Pocock's [skills](https://github.com/mattpocock/skills/tree/5863806dd4e15c63174bee4e7515a4256792ecfa)
1.3.2 (MIT; see [LICENSE](LICENSE)), pinned in `vendor.json`. The `engineering` and
`productivity` buckets are unmodified upstream bytes; refresh them with
`scripts/vendor.py`, never by editing. The frozen `misc`, `deprecated` and
`in-progress` buckets are left out, and `tdd` comes from [pstack](../phi9t-pstack/tdd/SKILL.md)
to keep one skill per name. Matt's `setup-matt-pocock-skills` writes the
`docs/agents/*` files this repository's AGENTS.md reads.

Repository instructions, permissions and landing policy outrank any skill here.
For phi9t_stack work, [phi9t](../phi9t/SKILL.md) owns workspaces and landing.
